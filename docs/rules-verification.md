# Synthesis rules: verified against the game

Ground truth comes from the live game (Steam v1.8.0.0) through the game reader in [`bridge/`](../bridge/) (`/api/synthesis`,
`bridge/analyze/watch_synth.py`; raw logs of the runs are in [`bridge/data/experiments/`](../bridge/data/experiments/)); the simulator side is `scripts/simulate.ts`, which uses the optimizer's
own scoring code. Coordinates are (row, col) on the board, 0-based from the top-left.

The effect line test (8, 9) covered all 55 settled states of a free-play run with 0–21 ice value and
0–9 ice links on 安寧藥膏 (lines: ice max 10 / restrict 3, ice max 6 / restrict 1, wind max 6).
Each line is tied to one ingredient row of the recipe (`itemrecipedata.xml`), not just to a color.

Full boards replayed through `scripts/simulate.ts` give exactly the game's per-element values and link
counts: `0,6,0,2,2` / links `0,2,0,0,0` (4 pieces) and `0,2,1,3,0` (bonus + overlap + "-2" cell).

## Still open

- Whether 3♥ gives Lv5 (only 2♥ → Lv3 and 4♥ → Lv5/Lv7 seen), and the 10-link "add material" skill.
- Whether the quality bonus per link (3) depends on friendship (seen 3 at 2♥ and at 4♥).
- Whether overlapping gives anything beyond replacement (the stacked cell shows a sparkle effect).
- What the "-2" cells are for. Whether full rows/columns change the great-success roll without showing it
  (would need many syntheses to measure).

End-to-end check: 布料 plan 1 from the optimizer (owned materials, full board, no overlap) was placed in game step by
step; quality went 89 → 162 (+73, as predicted), wind value 27 and both effects matched the plan.

The observations above are encoded as regression tests in `src/lib/optimizer/rules.test.ts` (`vp test`).
